ABOUT CAREQUEUE

A Smarter Way to Manage Hospital Consultations

CareQueue is an agentic AI-driven healthcare queue management system designed to make hospital visits faster, transparent, and more convenient for patients and doctors.

What is CareQueue?

CareQueue coordinates patient triage, symptom risk assessments, and smart queue prioritisation. Patients monitor their wait times remotely while medical staff enjoy streamlined consultation workflows.

Minimal Waiting

Real-time queue estimates and dynamic priority scheduling eliminate packed waiting rooms and unnecessary idle time.

Live Queue Tracking

Real-time token position updates inform patients when their turn approaches, with automatic SMS alerts.

Doctor Efficiency

Physicians easily view scheduled patients, manage high-priority emergency cases, and update consultation statuses in one click.

Why CareQueue?

Our mission is to create a seamless hospital experience for every patient. CareQueue bridges the gap between patient intake and doctor consultation with artificial intelligence and modern digital queueing.
